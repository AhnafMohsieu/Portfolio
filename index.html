03

About

I am a BBA candidate at United International University in Dhaka. My work sits between two disciplines: shipping software for small businesses and studying how technology spreads through finance research.

On the build side I focus on WordPress and WooCommerce storefronts for the Bangladeshi market, where bKash, Nagad, and cash on delivery matter more than card checkout, and on offline-first browser apps that keep working when the network does not. On the research side I care about reproducibility: the analyses behind my manuscripts run from versioned code and checked-in result files.

Toolkit WordPress and WooCommerce · vanilla JavaScript and PWAs · Chart.js · Python · LaTeX · RAG and LLM evaluation · bibliometric analysis · bKash, Nagad, and COD integration
